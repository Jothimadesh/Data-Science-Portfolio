# 🛒 HR Workforce Intelligence Engine

## **🎯 Business Problem**
HR teams need to track attrition trends, KPIs and workforce metrics in real time. 
This dashboard provides instant visibility into employee performance across departments, 
demographics and job roles.

## **📊 Dataset**
Source: IBM HR Analytics (Kaggle)  
Rows: 1,470 employees  
Columns: 35 features  
Domain: Human Resources  

## **🚀 Live Demo**
👉 [Click Here to Open Live Dashboard](http://localhost:8509/)

## **📈 Key Business Insights**
• Sales highest attrition → **26%**  
• R&D most stable → **14%** attrition  
• Lab Technicians highest turnover  
• Average salary → **$65,000/month**  
• Age 30-35 most at-risk group  
• Overtime workers → **2x** attrition risk

## **💡 Business Recommendations**
• Cap overtime to reduce attrition  
• Sales retention program needed  
• Salary review ages 30-35 group  
• Monitor Lab Technicians closely
